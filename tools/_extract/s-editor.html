Studying in Australia: a 2026 guide for international students

Australia hosts more than 600,000 international students each year, spread across universities, vocational colleges and English language centres. This guide walks through what has changed for the 2026 intake.

Choosing a course

Start with the field rather than the institution. Entry requirements vary far more by discipline than by university, and the recognised qualification frameworks differ between undergraduate and postgraduate study.

Applications for the February intake typically close in November, though some programmes accept late applications into December.
