Studying in Australia in 2026: what international students need to know

Australia remains one of the most popular destinations for international study, but the 2026 intake brings changes worth understanding before you apply.

Start with the field, not the university

Entry requirements differ far more between disciplines than between institutions. A nursing programme in Adelaide and one in Perth will ask for broadly similar prerequisites, while engineering and nursing at the same university will not.

Application timing

Most February intakes close applications in November. Some providers accept late applications into December, but scholarship deadlines rarely move.
